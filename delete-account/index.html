Account & Data Deletion

Account Deletion Request

PredIIQ gives users a clear way to request deletion of their account and associated app data. To begin, send an email using the details below.

Send your request to
tech@prediiq.com

Required email subject

Delete My Account

What to include

Please include your PredIIQ account email address so we can locate and process your request.

How it works

1
Email us at tech@prediiq.com.
2
Use the subject line Delete My Account.
3
Include your PredIIQ account email address.
4
Requests are processed within 48 hours.

Data deleted

  • Account email
  • User profile
  • Profile picture
  • Subscription/trial profile data
  • App account data stored in Firebase

Data retained

  • Payment records handled by Google Play or Apple may be retained according to their policies.
  • Legal or security records may be retained only if required by law.
