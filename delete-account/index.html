Account & Data Deletion

Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your PredIIQ account or specific data by contacting us.

Send your request to
support@prediiq.com

Email Subject

Delete My Account → deletes your full account
Delete My Data → deletes data only (account remains active)

What to include

Your PredIIQ account email address so we can process your request.

How it works

1
Email support@prediiq.com
2
Choose subject:
Delete My Account or Delete My Data
3
Include your account email
4
Processed within 48 hours

Data deleted

  • Account email
  • User profile
  • Profile picture
  • Subscription/trial data
  • Firebase stored data

Data retained

  • Payment records (Google Play / Apple)
  • Legal/security records if required
