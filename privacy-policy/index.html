PredIIQ collects and processes certain information necessary to operate the Application, maintain account security, provide football predictions, manage subscriptions, and improve application performance.

This information helps us maintain application functionality, investigate technical issues, protect our services, prevent misuse, and improve reliability.

PredIIQ does not intentionally collect precise GPS location information.

Free Trials and Device Verification

PredIIQ may offer eligible users a limited free trial of premium features.

To maintain fair access and prevent repeated or unauthorized trial claims, PredIIQ uses account-based and device-related eligibility verification.

These security measures help determine whether a free trial has previously been claimed and whether the Application is operating in an authorized environment.

Android Devices

On Android, PredIIQ uses Google Play Integrity to help verify application authenticity, device integrity, and eligibility for protected application features.

We may process device-related identifiers, integrity verification results, and trial eligibility records to detect repeated trial claims, prevent unauthorized access, and protect the integrity of our subscription services.

Apple iOS Devices

On iOS, PredIIQ uses Apple's DeviceCheck technology to help determine whether a device has previously received a free trial.

DeviceCheck supports limited device eligibility verification through Apple's security infrastructure.

These technologies are used for application security, fraud prevention, and fair trial administration.

PredIIQ does not use these verification processes to access your personal photographs, contacts, private messages, or personal files.
